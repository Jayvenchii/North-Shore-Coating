Driveway sealcoating in Lincolnshire, IL: HOAs, neighbors and scheduling

By North Shore Coating · Local guide

If your Lincolnshire neighborhood has a homeowners association, check its guidelines before scheduling sealcoating. Some associations have rules about appearance, work hours or contractor access. Beyond that, the job is the same as anywhere: good prep, the right weather and about two days off the driveway.

Questions to check with an HOA

  • Is approval needed for driveway maintenance?
  • Are there allowed work days or hours?
  • Are there rules about street parking while the driveway cures?
  • Does the association have a preferred finish or appearance standard?

Coordinating with neighbors

When several neighbors seal around the same time, scheduling is simpler and the street looks consistent. It's also easier to plan street parking together. If you share a driveway or a common apron, agree on the date and how you'll split the cost before booking.

Prep that matters in newer subdivisions

Newer driveways are usually in good shape, so prep is mostly cleaning, crisp edging along lawns and concrete, and treating oil spots. Every sealcoating job we do includes all three.

Timing

Late spring through early fall, on a dry day of at least 50°F and rising. In warm, dry weather most driveways take foot traffic after about 24 hours and cars after about 48, longer in shade or cool weather. See the best time to sealcoat in Lake County.

Getting the street to look consistent

In neighborhoods where driveways sit side by side, a freshly sealed driveway next to a faded one stands out. Some neighbors choose to schedule around the same time so the street looks even and street parking is planned once. If you do, each driveway is still its own job with its own prep, especially around oil spots and edges.

Day-of reminders

  • Move every car the night before, including any in the garage.
  • Turn off irrigation for 24 hours before and after.
  • Keep pets and kids off until the surface is dry.
  • Let landscapers know the date so mowers stay off.
