Christmas lights FAQ

What’s included in Christmas light installation?

Everything from start to finish: we design a lighting plan for your home or business, install the lights, take them down after the holidays and store them until next season.

Do I need to get on a ladder or help with anything?

No. Our crew handles all of the ladder work, roofline and the installation itself. You just enjoy the lights.

How much does Christmas light installation cost?

Every home is different, so pricing depends on things like the length of your rooflines, the height of the house and the design you choose. Request a free quote and include a few photos of your home if you can.

When should I book?

The earlier the better. Requesting your quote early gives you the most installation dates to choose from before the holidays.

Do you install lights on commercial properties?

Yes. We work with both homes and businesses. Choose “Commercial” on the quote form and tell us about your property.

When do you take the lights down?

After the holiday season, we come back to take everything down carefully, so you don’t have to deal with it in the cold.

What happens to the lights after the season?

We store them for you until next year, so there’s nothing to untangle or find room for in your garage.

Which areas do you serve?

Homes and businesses across the Chicago North Shore and Lake County, Illinois. See the list of towns below, or call 773-530-4888 if you don’t see yours.
