Why sealcoat your driveway?

Road salt, snowmelt and summer sun are hard on asphalt. UV light dries out the binder that holds the stone together, so a blacktop driveway slowly fades from black to gray and turns brittle. Salty water soaks into the surface, and every freeze-thaw cycle pushes small cracks a little wider.

Residential driveway sealcoating puts a protective layer back on top. It fills the tiny pores in the surface so water runs off instead of soaking in, resists oil and gas spills, and shields the asphalt from the sun. Before any sealer goes down, we edge, clean and treat oil spots so the coating bonds to bare asphalt.

A fresh coat every two to three years keeps your driveway deep black, slows cracking and loose stone, and pushes back the day you need resurfacing or a full replacement, both of which cost far more than sealing. It's one of the simplest ways to protect your home's curb appeal on the North Shore.

Driveway sealcoating FAQ

How much does driveway sealcoating cost?

The price depends mostly on the size of your driveway, the condition of the surface and how much prep it needs, such as edging, cleaning, oil spot treatment or repairs. We measure your driveway by satellite and send a free written estimate, usually within 24 hours.

What is the typical price range for a residential driveway?

Most residential driveway sealcoating projects range from $250–$550. Your final price depends on driveway size, surface condition, preparation, and any additional repairs needed. Request a free estimate for pricing specific to your driveway.

How long does sealcoating take to dry before walking or driving on it?

In warm, dry weather, most driveways are ready for foot traffic after about 24 hours and for cars after about 48 hours. Cool temperatures, humidity, shade and the sealer manufacturer's instructions can all stretch that, so we'll give you the timing for your driveway on the day of the job.

How should I prepare my driveway before sealcoating?

Move all vehicles off the driveway before we arrive, turn off sprinklers for 24 hours before and after, and keep pets and kids off until it's dry. You don't need to be home. We take care of edging, cleaning and oil spot treatment.

How long does driveway sealcoating last?

A properly applied coat usually protects a residential driveway for about 2 to 3 years. Sun, traffic, snow plowing and road salt all affect how fast it wears, so some driveways need attention a little sooner than others.

How often should I sealcoat my driveway?

Every 2 to 3 years for most driveways. Sealing every year can build up too much coating, which can crack and peel. If your driveway doesn't need it yet, we'll tell you.

What time of year is best for sealcoating in Illinois?

Late spring through early fall, roughly May to September in the Chicago area. Sealer needs dry pavement, temperatures of at least 50°F and rising, and no rain for 24 hours, and the product's own temperature limits apply too. Sealing in early fall protects your driveway before salt season.

Does sealcoating repair cracks or potholes?

No. Sealcoating covers fine hairline cracks along with the rest of the surface, but it doesn't fill or repair wider cracks, potholes or crumbling areas. Those need repair before sealing. We only do sealcoating, so if your driveway needs repair first, we'll tell you in your estimate.

How long does the service take?

Most residential driveways are prepped and sealed in a single visit, usually within a day. Larger driveways can take longer, and if rain is in the forecast we'll reschedule rather than risk the finish.
