Driveway sealcoating in Zion, IL: a simple 10-year maintenance plan

By North Shore Coating · Local guide

A well-maintained asphalt driveway can last for decades. Over ten years, the plan is simple: look it over every spring, keep cracks from spreading, and sealcoat about every two to three years. That's roughly three to four coats per decade.

The 10-year plan

WhenWhat to do
Every springWalk the driveway. Check color, edges, cracks and oil stains.
Year 1Sealcoat if it hasn't been done in 2–3 years.
Year 3 or 4Reseal. Repair any wide cracks first.
Year 6 or 7Reseal. Check edges and low spots.
Year 9 or 10Reseal, or assess for resurfacing if damage has spread.

Small habits that add years

  • Fix vehicle leaks and blot spills quickly.
  • Keep gutters and downspouts directing water away.
  • Use de-icer sparingly and keep plow blades raised.
  • Keep edges trimmed so water drains off the side.

When the plan changes

If you see alligator cracking, sinking or potholes, the driveway needs repair before more sealing. Read sealing vs. resurfacing.

What each coat protects against

ThreatHow sealing helps
Sun (UV)Blocks UV that dries out the binder.
Water and freeze-thawSheds water so less soaks in and freezes.
Road saltKeeps salty melt on the surface instead of in it.
Oil and gasSlows spills from softening the asphalt.

Keeping track

The easiest way to stick to a plan is to write it down. Note the month and year of each coat and any repairs, and check the driveway each spring. If you'd rather not track it yourself, ask us to remind you when it's time.

Tip: photos from the same spot each spring make it easy to see whether the driveway is holding up or starting to slip.
