Driveway sealcoating in Northfield, IL: a spring checklist after winter

By North Shore Coating · Local guide

The best time to decide whether your driveway needs sealing is right after the snow melts. Winter damage is easy to see, and booking early gets you a better spot on the schedule. Here's a simple walk-through for Northfield homeowners.

The 10-minute spring walk

  1. Color. Has the surface faded to gray or brown?
  2. Edges. Are they cracking, crumbling or pulling away from the lawn?
  3. Street end. Is there loose stone or salt residue where plows hit?
  4. Garage end. Are oil stains bigger than last fall?
  5. Cracks. Are there new hairline cracks? Any wide ones you can see into?
  6. Water test. Spray a section with a hose. Does it bead or soak in?

Two or more yes answers usually means it's time to seal this year. Our list of signs your driveway needs sealcoating goes deeper.

Sort cracks into two groups

Thin, separate hairline cracks are a normal sign of aging, and sealing slows them down. Wide cracks, web-like alligator cracking, sinking and potholes need repair first. Sealer is a protective coating, not a filler. See can you seal a driveway with cracks?

Clean up the winter mess

Sweep away sand and salt residue, and rinse the surface. That alone makes the driveway easier to judge. Before sealing, we handle edging, full cleaning and oil spot treatment as part of every sealcoating job.

Don't seal on the first warm day

Sealer needs dry pavement, temperatures of at least 50°F and rising, and about 24 hours without rain. Early spring nights are often too cold. Late spring through early fall is the window, and booking in spring gets you on the calendar before it fills.
