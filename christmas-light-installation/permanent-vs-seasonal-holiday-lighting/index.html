Permanent vs. seasonal holiday lighting: which is best for your Lake County home?

By North Shore Coating · 3 min read

Permanent lighting is a one-time investment that stays on your home year-round; seasonal lighting is installed for the holidays and taken down after. Permanent systems offer app-controlled color for every occasion, while seasonal displays give you the classic warm-white Christmas look with no permanent hardware on your house.

Both are popular in Lake County. Here's how they compare.

What permanent holiday lighting is

Permanent systems, such as Trimlight and Jellyfish Lighting, mount LED lights inside a low-profile track installed along your roofline or soffits. The track is designed to blend in during the day. At night, you control colors, patterns and schedules from an app, for Christmas, game days, birthdays or simple everyday accent lighting.

What seasonal holiday lighting is

Seasonal displays are installed before the holidays and removed afterward. A professional service handles the design, installation, takedown and storage. You get the traditional look of bright bulbs along rooflines, wrapped trees and lit doorways, with nothing left on your home the rest of the year.

Which is right for you?

Choose permanent lighting if

  • You plan to stay in your home for many years.
  • You want lighting for more than just the holidays.
  • You're comfortable with a larger one-time investment.

Choose seasonal installation if

  • You want the classic Christmas look, including trees, bushes and garland.
  • You'd rather not have hardware on your roofline all year.
  • You want a lower cost each year with no long-term commitment.

The Lake County factor

Long winters, lake wind and heavy tree cover in towns like Lake Forest and Highland Park mean any system needs secure mounting and protected connections. Seasonal displays also let you change your design from year to year, and wrapping mature trees is something a roofline track can't do.

Questions to ask before you decide

  • How long do you plan to stay in your home?
  • Do you want lighting only for Christmas, or for other occasions too?
  • Do you want trees, bushes and garland lit, or just the roofline?
  • Are you comfortable with hardware mounted on your home year-round?
  • What's your budget this year versus over the next five years?

Cost over time

Permanent systems ask for a larger investment upfront and then run for years. Seasonal installation is paid each season, which keeps the yearly cost lower and lets you change your design as your home, family and taste change. Many homeowners start with seasonal lighting to see what they like before considering anything permanent.

The look from the street

Permanent track lighting tends to read as clean points of light along the roofline. A seasonal display adds dimension: wrapped trees, lit bushes and glowing entries that give a home the traditional Christmas feel many families want.
