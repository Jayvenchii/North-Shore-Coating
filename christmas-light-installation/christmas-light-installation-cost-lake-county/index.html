How much does professional Christmas light installation cost in Lake County? (2026 guide)

By North Shore Coating · 3 min read

Most homeowners pay a few hundred dollars for a basic roofline and well over a thousand for a large, detailed display. National cost guides put the average professional Christmas light installation at about $440, with most jobs between roughly $220 and $680, or about $2.50 to $7 per linear foot. Your home's size, roof complexity and design are what move you up or down that range.

If you're searching for Christmas light installation in Lake County, IL, here's how pricing actually works, so you can compare quotes with confidence.

What changes the cost of your installation

Home size and linear footage

Roofline length is the biggest factor. A ranch with one simple roofline takes far less material and time than a two-story colonial with multiple gables and a garage.

Roof height and complexity

Steep pitches, second- and third-story eaves and lots of peaks and valleys take more time and more careful safety setup. That's exactly the work most homeowners would rather not do themselves.

Trees, bushes and extra elements

Wrapping tree trunks and branches, lighting bushes, outlining windows and doors, and adding garland all add to the design and the total.

Power and custom wiring

Where your outlets are, how many circuits are available and whether runs need to be custom-fit to your home all affect the labor involved. A clean install hides cords and connections instead of draping them across the yard.

Install only, or full service

Some quotes cover installation only. Full service adds takedown after the holidays and storage until next year, which is usually the better value once you count your own time in January.

Why a lowball quote can cost more

  • Lights clipped loosely can sag, blow off in Lake County wind or fail during the first cold snap.
  • Extension cords run across walkways are a tripping and moisture hazard.
  • If takedown isn't included, you're back on the ladder in January.

When comparing professional Christmas light hanging services, ask what's included: design, installation, takedown, storage, and what happens if a section goes out mid-season.

Is it worth it to hire someone to put up Christmas lights?

For most busy households, yes. You avoid a cold weekend on a ladder, the display looks cleaner and more even, and you don't have to store tangled bins all year. For a two-story home, the safety benefit alone is worth it.
