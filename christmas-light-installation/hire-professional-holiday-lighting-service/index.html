5 reasons to skip the ladder and hire a professional holiday lighting service this year

By North Shore Coating · 3 min read

Hanging Christmas lights means hours on a ladder in the cold, often on a steep or icy roofline. Hiring a professional holiday lighting service takes the risk, the time and the takedown off your plate, and the result usually looks better. Here are five reasons more Lake County homeowners hire someone to put up their Christmas lights.

1. Ladders and cold rooflines are a real risk

Every year, emergency rooms see holiday decorating injuries, and falls from ladders are among the most serious. Add frost, wind off the lake and early darkness, and a two-story roofline is no place for a weekend project. Professional installers work at height every day, with the right ladders, safety setup and experience.

The honest test: if you'd feel uneasy standing on the top half of a ladder at your second-story gutter in 30-degree weather, that's a job to hand off.

2. You get your weekends back

Between untangling strands, testing bulbs, running to the store for replacements and actually hanging everything, a DIY display can eat a full weekend. Then it all comes down again in January. With a professional Christmas light hanging service, you get those days back for family, travel or simply enjoying the season.

3. The display simply looks better

Professionals plan the design around your home. Lines run straight and evenly spaced, corners are clean, and cords are hidden instead of draped across the lawn. That polished look is the difference between "they put up lights" and the house everyone slows down for.

4. Takedown and storage are handled

The worst part of holiday lights is January: freezing hands, tangled strands and bulky bins in the garage. With full-service residential Christmas light installation, takedown after the holidays and storage until next year are part of the plan.

5. Problems get fixed without you climbing up

If a section goes dark in mid-December, you shouldn't have to find the ladder. A full-service installer comes back to fix it, so your home stays lit through the season.

How to choose holiday lighting installers near you

  • Ask exactly what's included: design, install, takedown and storage.
  • Ask how lights are attached so nothing damages shingles or gutters.
  • Ask how cords and connections are protected from snow and rain.
  • Get a written quote with a clear scope.
