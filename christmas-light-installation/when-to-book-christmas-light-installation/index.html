When should you book your Christmas light installation in the Chicago suburbs?

By North Shore Coating · 3 min read

Book your Christmas light installation between September and early November. That's when you get the widest choice of installation dates and the mildest weather for the crew. By late November, schedules tighten and temperatures in the Chicago suburbs regularly drop below freezing.

Why booking early matters

Installation dates fill up

Most homeowners want their lights up by Thanksgiving weekend or the first week of December. Professional holiday lighting installers can only work so many homes per day, so the most popular dates go first.

Weather gets harder

Installing in mild fall weather is safer and faster than working on frosty rooflines in December. Booking early means your display goes up before ice and snow become a factor.

Lights on, switched off until you're ready

An early install doesn't mean an early display. Lights can go up in November and stay off until you want them on.

What if it's already late in the season?

It's still worth asking. Openings come up, and smaller displays can sometimes fit in later. The sooner you reach out, the better your options.

Tips for a smooth installation

  • Know where your outdoor outlets are, and whether any are on a timer.
  • Send photos of the front of your home with your quote request.
  • Tell us about anything you want lit specifically, like a favorite tree.

Why the Chicago suburbs need an early start

Winter arrives early around Lake County and the North Shore. Hard freezes often begin in November, and snow and ice can come before Thanksgiving. Installation is safer and faster on dry, mild days, which is why the best dates go first.

What happens when you wait

  • Fewer dates to choose from. Late requests get whatever openings are left.
  • Weather delays. Rain, ice and snow can push installs back.
  • Less time to enjoy it. A December install means fewer nights with your home lit.

Booking for a business

Commercial properties often need approval from a landlord, property manager or association. Starting in September or October leaves time for approvals and still gets the display up before the busy holiday shopping weeks.
