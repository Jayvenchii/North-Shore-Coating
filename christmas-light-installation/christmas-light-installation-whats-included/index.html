What's included in full-service holiday lighting? (Install, takedown, maintenance & storage)

By North Shore Coating · 3 min read

Full-service holiday lighting covers everything from design to storage: we plan your display, install it, keep it working through the season, take it down after the holidays and store it until next year. You never fix a burnt-out bulb in December or wrestle bulky bins into the garage in January.

1. Design

Every home is different. We plan which rooflines, windows, doors, trees and bushes to light so the display looks balanced from the street and fits your home's architecture.

2. Professional installation

Our crew handles all of the ladder work. Lights are fitted to your rooflines, attached securely without damaging shingles or gutters, and wired cleanly so cords aren't draped across your yard. This is residential Christmas light installation done the way you'd want it done, without you lifting a finger.

3. Maintenance during the season

If a strand or section goes dark in the middle of December, you call us. We come back and fix it, so your home stays lit right through the holidays.

What this means for you: no ladders, no hunting for replacement bulbs and no half-lit roofline during the week of your holiday party.

4. Takedown after the holidays

When the season ends, we return to take everything down carefully. That's the cold, tedious part of holiday lighting most homeowners dread, and it's done for you.

5. Storage until next year

Instead of tangled bins taking up space in your garage or basement, your display is stored until the next season.

Why full service is worth it

The installation is only part of the work. Most homeowners who have done their own lights remember the rest: testing strands on the living room floor, replacing bulbs in the cold, climbing back up when a section fails a week before a party, and wrestling everything back into bins in January. Full-service residential Christmas light installation removes every one of those steps.

It also means your display looks the same from the first night to the last. Lights that sag, blink out or blow loose in the wind get fixed instead of ignored until spring. For many families, that consistency is the real value: a home that looks finished and cared for all season long.
