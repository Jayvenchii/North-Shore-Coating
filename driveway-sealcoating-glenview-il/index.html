Driveway sealcoating in Glenview, IL: keeping a newer driveway looking new

By North Shore Coating · Local guide

A driveway between about 3 and 15 years old gets the most value from sealcoating. The surface is still sound, so each coat protects asphalt that's worth protecting. Skip those years and the same driveway can need patching or resurfacing much sooner.

Why the middle years matter most

New asphalt starts out flexible and tight. Over the first decade, sun dries out the binder, water finds small openings, and freeze-thaw cycles widen them. Sealcoating during those years slows each of those steps. Once cracks have spread across the surface, sealer can't reverse them.

A simple maintenance schedule

Driveway ageWhat to do
0–1 yearLet new asphalt cure. Ask your paver when to first seal.
1–2 yearsFirst sealcoat once the color starts to fade.
Every 2–3 yearsReseal. Check each spring for new cracks and oil stains.
Any timeRepair wide cracks or potholes before the next coat.

Don't over-seal

Sealing every year stacks coats faster than they wear, which leads to peeling and flaking. If the last coat is still even and water still beads, wait another season. More in how often to sealcoat.

Coal tar and Cook County

Suburban Cook County passed a ban on coal tar and other high-PAH pavement sealants in 2024. Asphalt emulsion is the standard. See coal tar vs. asphalt emulsion.

What wears a mid-life driveway

  • Sun dries the binder and fades the color.
  • Daily traffic wears the area by the garage and the street.
  • Snow plowing scrapes the surface and edges every winter.
  • Road salt keeps the surface wet through freeze-thaw cycles.
  • Oil drips soften the asphalt where cars park.

Each coat of sealer takes that wear so the asphalt doesn't. When the coat wears thin, usually after two to three years, it's time for the next one.

Signs it's time for the next coat

Spray part of the driveway with a hose. If water beads, the coat is still working. If it soaks in and darkens the surface, it's time. Gray patches near the garage, loose grit at the street end and new hairline cracks are other signs. See 5 signs your driveway needs sealcoating.

Tip: write the month and year on a note in your garage each time the driveway is sealed. It makes the schedule easy to follow.
