How to get ready for sealcoating day

By North Shore Coating · 7 min read

Most of the work on sealcoating day belongs to the crew. Still, a few small things on your end help residential driveway sealcoating go smoothly, keep the finish clean and make sure nothing gets in the way. None of it takes long, and most of it can be done the night before.

Here's a simple checklist for the day before and the morning of, a walkthrough of what a good driveway sealcoating contractor does while they're there, what to do after they leave, and answers to the questions homeowners ask most. Bookmark it, or send it to whoever else in your household uses the driveway.

The day before sealcoating

  • Turn off sprinklers. Keep them off for 24 hours before and after the job. Water on fresh sealer can ruin the finish.
  • Clear the driveway. Move basketball hoops, planters, bikes, toys, grills and trash bins off the surface.
  • Check your trash day. If pickup falls during the 48-hour cure time, put bins at the curb on the street side or in the grass.
  • Tell the household. Make sure everyone knows the driveway will be closed for about two days, including anyone who parks there or expects deliveries.

If you have a landscaper or lawn service, let them know the date too. Mowers and trimmers throwing clippings onto wet sealer, or a crew walking across it, are among the most common causes of marks on a fresh coat. Rescheduling them by a day is an easy fix.

The morning of

  • Move every vehicle off the driveway. If you'll need a car in the next 48 hours, park it on the street, not in the garage. A car in the garage stays there until the sealer has cured.
  • Keep pets inside so they don't walk through wet sealer and track it onto floors.
  • Close the garage door to keep dust and sealer out.
  • Unlock any gates the crew will need to get through.

You don't need to be home. Many homeowners are at work while their driveway is sealed. As long as the driveway is clear, the crew can do everything without you, and a good local sealcoating company will let you know when the job is finished and when the driveway is ready to use.

What happens on sealcoating day

  1. Edge along the sides so grass and dirt are cut back from the asphalt.
  2. Clean the surface with brooms and blowers to remove dust, debris and loose stone.
  3. Treat oil and gas stains with primer so the sealer bonds over them.
  4. Cut in the edges by hand to keep sealer off concrete, sidewalks and landscaping.
  5. Apply two thin coats of sealer for even, lasting coverage.
  6. Block off the driveway so no one drives on it by mistake.

Steps one through four are the prep, and they matter more than the sealer itself. A driveway coating can only bond to clean, sound asphalt. When you compare contractors, ask which of these steps are included, because skipping the prep is the most common way low-priced asphalt driveway sealing jobs cut corners.

After the crew leaves

  • Stay off the driveway on foot for about 24 hours.
  • Wait 48 hours before driving or parking on it.
  • Keep sprinklers off for another 24 hours.
  • Roll straight in and out for the first few days and avoid turning the wheel while parked.
  • Leave the barricade up until you're ready to use the driveway again.

Cool, cloudy or humid weather can stretch those times a little, and shady sections usually cure last. If you're unsure, check the shadiest part of the driveway before you drive on it. Your blacktop driveway will look its best about a week after the job, once the coating has fully hardened.

Common sealcoating day questions

Will sealer get on my garage floor?

Not if you wait the full 48 hours. Tracking almost always happens when someone drives or walks on the driveway too early, before the sealer has cured.

What about the concrete apron or sidewalk?

The edges are cut in by hand to keep sealer on the asphalt and off concrete, brick pavers and landscaping.

What if it rains?

The forecast is checked before the job, and the work is rescheduled if rain is expected within 24 hours. Moving a date is much better than a washed-out finish.

Does sealcoating smell?

Asphalt-emulsion sealer has a mild smell while it dries that fades within a day or so. Keeping garage doors and nearby windows closed during the job helps.
