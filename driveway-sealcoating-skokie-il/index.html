Driveway sealcoating in Skokie, IL: what a good quote should include

By North Shore Coating · Local guide

A good sealcoating quote tells you exactly what will be done before any sealer goes down: edging, cleaning, oil spot treatment, how many coats and what product. If a quote is just a single number with no details, you can't tell whether it's a fair price or a shortcut.

What a complete quote covers

  • Edging grass and soil back from the asphalt.
  • Cleaning the full surface so sealer bonds.
  • Oil spot treatment where cars park.
  • Number of coats and how they're applied.
  • Type of sealer. In Cook County, it shouldn't be coal tar.
  • Weather policy if rain shows up in the forecast.
  • Honest notes about any area that needs repair first.

Red flags

  • Door-to-door crews with "leftover material" from a nearby job.
  • A same-day discount that expires if you don't decide now.
  • No address, phone number or written estimate.
  • Sealing over wide cracks or potholes without mentioning them.

Coal tar is banned in suburban Cook County

Cook County passed an ordinance in 2024 banning coal tar and other high-PAH sealants in suburban Cook County, which includes Skokie. Asphalt-emulsion sealer is the standard alternative. Read more in coal tar vs. asphalt emulsion.

Why prep is where quotes differ

Sealer is the visible part of the job, but prep decides how long it lasts. Sealer over dirt, grass clippings or oil doesn't bond and starts wearing within weeks. That's the most common reason a cheap job needs redoing in a year.
