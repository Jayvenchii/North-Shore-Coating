Driveway sealcoating in Gurnee, IL: what happens on sealcoating day

By North Shore Coating · Local guide

Most residential driveways are prepped and sealed in a single visit, usually within a day. Most of the time goes into preparation. Then the driveway needs about 24 hours before foot traffic and about 48 before cars in warm, dry weather.

Step by step

  1. Walk-through. A quick look for oil spots, cracks and borders that need care.
  2. Edging. Grass and soil are cut back to the true edge of the asphalt.
  3. Cleaning. Dirt, leaves and debris come off so sealer can bond.
  4. Oil spot treatment. Stains get primed so the coat won't peel there.
  5. Sealing. Sealer is applied evenly, with careful work along edges and concrete.
  6. Blocking off. The driveway is marked so nobody drives on it early.

What you need to do

Move every car, turn off sprinklers for 24 hours before and after, and keep pets and kids inside. You don't need to be home. Full list: how to get ready for sealcoating day.

The first week

Avoid turning the wheel while the car is stopped, and keep heavy trucks off if you can. Light tire marks are common and fade.

Common questions on the day

Do I need to be home? No. Just have the cars moved and the sprinklers off.

Will it smell? Asphalt-emulsion sealers have a mild odor that fades as they cure. Keep windows near the driveway closed during application if you're sensitive to smells.

What if it rains? If rain is forecast within about 24 hours, the job is rescheduled.

When can I walk on it? In warm, dry weather, usually after about 24 hours. Shade, humidity and cool nights stretch that.

After the job

  • Leave the barriers up until the driveway is ready.
  • Check the shadiest section before driving on it.
  • Keep sprinklers off for 24 hours.
  • Expect the color to settle into a deep, even black over the first week.
